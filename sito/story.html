OUR STORY

The bar of the future speaks Roman.

A spark born behind a bar counter in Rome, an idea that grew between two friends, an app built from scratch between Italy and the United States. How Maicol Chiappetta and Kevin Savinetti turned their experience into a business.

Every bar has two lives. The one guests see: the lights, the music, the cocktail that lands perfectly at the right moment. And the one nobody sees: inventory, costs, shifts, bottles to count and numbers that have to add up. That is where, behind the scenes, a bar either grows or closes. And that is where Barback begins.

01The spark

Maicol Chiappetta knows that second life by heart. First a bartender, then a bar manager, he spent years behind the counters of some of Rome's best-known venues, learning that the perfect cocktail is only the visible part of the job. The rest lives on paper, in group chats and in people's heads. And one question had been on his mind for a long time: why had nobody ever built a tool truly designed for the people on the other side of the bar?

The spark came from him. But it was when he shared it with his friend Kevin Savinetti that it became something bigger: an idea belonging to both of them, discussed, taken apart and rebuilt together until it became a real project.

02The man of two continents

Kevin was born in Rome, but his path took him far away. Oregon, California, Las Vegas, New York, Miami: years in American hospitality, across kitchens, bars and management, in cities where a venue lives or dies by its numbers. There he saw what happens when technology truly becomes part of the trade.

By day, service, the floor, the numbers. By night, in his free time, code. Kevin built Barback from scratch, one line at a time: the architecture, the database, the app, the website, the sales materials. From zero to a thousand, no shortcuts.

Not office software bent to fit a bar. The bar itself, as software.

03A bar in your pocket

The result is a management app designed by people who live the bar, and built to fit in your phone. Everyone logs in with their own PIN and sees only what they need: the bartender counts the empties, the bar manager checks stock and costs, the owner sees everything.

The drink cost of every cocktail is calculated from the recipe, millilitre by millilitre. When a bottle drops below its par level, the app prepares the supplier order and sends it on WhatsApp with one tap. And anyone requesting time off can see right away whether it leaves a shift uncovered.

04The launch

On 23 October 2026 Barback hit the market, taken straight into venues by a dedicated sales network. One platform for many bars, each with its own separate data, and a website in Italian, English and Spanish. Behind it is ART& CREATION LLC, the American company founded by Kevin.

The next chapter is written one bar at a time. The goal is simple to say and hard to do: that at the end of the night, behind every counter, the numbers add up in ten minutes. And that the people who work in bars can get back to what they love most: their guests.
